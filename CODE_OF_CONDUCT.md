# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Participants should be constructive, assume good intent, respect different
viewpoints and experience levels, and focus criticism on the work rather than
the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
language or imagery, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing
someone's private information, or other conduct that a reasonable person would
consider hostile or unsafe.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the
project in public spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, review
comments, events, and project-related private communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact path in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and clearly label the report as a code-of-conduct
matter. Project maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter
privacy where possible, and may edit or remove content, issue warnings, limit
participation, or permanently ban participants when appropriate.

Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report is prohibited.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers are responsible for applying this policy fairly and consistently.
They will consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of the
community when deciding on a response.
